## The Rook
- **Role:** Villain. A greedy warlord who wants to be the only boss in the region and sees himself as a king, the "King of the Ashwood". Leads the Crows from Ashwatch, an old toll fort deep in the Ashwood.
- **Description:** Not seen until Act 3. His toll and his crow-feather tokens (a black feather through a copper ring stamped with a squat tower) are everywhere on the road.
- **Appears in:** [Guarding the Caravan](chapters/01-the-caravan/adventures/01-guarding-the-caravan.md), [The Bandit Lord](chapters/03-the-bandit-lord/encounters/02-the-bandit-lord.md)

## Sable
- **Role:** The Rook's lieutenant. Leads the ambush that kills Tobin Marsh.
- **Description:** Cold and precise. A bandit captain with a hand crossbow. Carries the Crows' toll ledger.
- **Appears in:** [Attack 2: The Midway Ambush](chapters/01-the-caravan/encounters/02-the-midway-ambush.md), [Attack 3: Before Harrowgate](chapters/01-the-caravan/encounters/03-before-harrowgate.md)

## Tobin Marsh
- **Role:** Coach driver who hires the party. Killed at Crow's Ford.
- **Description:** Weathered, stubborn, and kind. Hester Marsh's cousin. Refuses to pay the Rook's toll.
- **Appears in:** [Guarding the Caravan](chapters/01-the-caravan/adventures/01-guarding-the-caravan.md), [Attack 2: The Midway Ambush](chapters/01-the-caravan/encounters/02-the-midway-ambush.md)

## Hester Marsh
- **Role:** Head of Marsh Freight in Harrowgate. Pays the party, rewards them for how they treated Tobin's body, and with Captain Vane asks them to investigate the Crows.
- **Description:** Tobin's cousin. Clever, practical, grieving. Wants the road back, not revenge.
- **Appears in:** [Following the Trail](chapters/02-the-trail/adventures/01-following-the-trail.md#1-arrival-in-harrowgate)

## Gully
- **Role:** Crow who demands the toll outside Millbrook.
- **Description:** A big, cheerful, not very bright tough.
- **Appears in:** [Attack 1: Outside Millbrook](chapters/01-the-caravan/encounters/01-outside-millbrook.md)

## Captain Roderick Vane
- **Role:** Captain of the Harrowgate guard. With Hester, asks the party to investigate the Crows, and makes them deputies.
- **Description:** Sixties, grey-bearded, limps from an old wound. Honest, tired, and blunt.
- **Appears in:** [Following the Trail](chapters/02-the-trail/adventures/01-following-the-trail.md#3-the-morning-meeting)

## Morwen Tull
- **Role:** Keeper of the Iron Harrow inn, and a source of rumors.
- **Description:** Talkative and well-informed; keeps the best rooms for guests who tip.
- **Appears in:** [Following the Trail](chapters/02-the-trail/adventures/01-following-the-trail.md#2-the-night-at-the-iron-harrow)

## Jory Fennick
- **Role:** Young Crow prisoner in the Harrowgate jail. Tells the party about Brannoc's camp if they win the skill challenge.
- **Description:** Seventeen, skinny, scared. Son of old Fennick the stablemaster.
- **Appears in:** [Following the Trail](chapters/02-the-trail/adventures/01-following-the-trail.md#4-the-prisoner)

## Brannoc, "the Magpie"
- **Role:** Crow lieutenant who runs the forward camp in the Ashwood. Knows what the Rook wants and where his fort is, if the party keeps him alive.
- **Description:** Forties, lean, neat black beard, fingers full of stolen rings. Vain and greedy; fears the Rook, but talks to save his life.
- **Appears in:** [The Crow Camp](chapters/02-the-trail/encounters/02-the-crow-camp.md), [Following the Trail](chapters/02-the-trail/adventures/01-following-the-trail.md#5-into-the-ashwood)
