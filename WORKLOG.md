# Work log

The Campaign Agent adds an entry at the end of each working session: what
was done, what was decided and why, and ideas set aside for later. Newest
entries come first.

## 2026-10-22 (second session)

- Pushed the campaign to GitHub as its own public repository (Rees-Brad/Bandit-2) and pointed the Campaign-Test collection's submodule at it.
- Moved the arrival in Harrowgate from Act 1 to the start of Act 2. Hester's bonus now depends on what the party did with Tobin's body: carried home (25 gp + 150 XP each), buried (10 gp each), or left (nothing). Added the Iron Harrow inn, its rumors, and Captain Roderick Vane.
- Wrote Act 2's town phase as a prisoner skill challenge (3 successes before 2 failures, Persuasion or Intimidation DC 13), then the Bear (Low) and Brannoc's Crow Camp (Moderate).
- Decisions: Brannoc is a Crow lieutenant (Warrior Veteran). The party learns about the Rook by keeping Brannoc alive or finding his hidden stash. The Rook just wants to be the only boss in the region and sees himself as a king, so the council-protection plot was dropped. His base moved from a hill pass to Ashwatch, an old fort in the woods, and Acts 1 and 2 were updated to match. The Lookout Post is kept, on the road to Ashwatch.
- Set aside: a rival bandit chief or a Crow deserter as the camp's leader.

## 2026-10-22

- Set the premise: a short drop-in adventure for level 3, in three acts (caravan guard, the trail, the bandit lord), about one session each, with a generic setting whose names DMs can rename.
- Wrote the overview and outline in `campaign.md`, and created the three chapters with placeholder sections for Acts 2 and 3.
- Wrote Act 1 in full: the adventure, three Crow attacks (outside Millbrook, at Crow's Ford, and before Harrowgate), and a mostly-combat road encounter table. Stat blocks were checked against the SRD 5.2.1 PDF.
- Decisions: no hidden patron behind the bandits. The Rook is a greedy warlord who wants to control all trade, kept simple so he drops into any campaign. Tobin dies at the midpoint, which gives Act 2 a personal motive. Sable's Pistol was swapped for a Hand Crossbow to fit settings without firearms.
- Set aside: a corrupt official or rival merchant as a secret patron (DMs can add one using the hooks in `campaign.md`), and the optional night raid (replaced by the three attacks).
