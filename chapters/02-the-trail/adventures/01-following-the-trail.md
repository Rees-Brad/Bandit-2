# Following the Trail

**Act 2. One session. Four or five characters of level 3.**

The party reaches Harrowgate with the caravan and meets Tobin's cousin, Hester Marsh. How she receives them depends on what they did with Tobin's body. After a night at the Iron Harrow inn, Hester and the captain of the town guard ask the party to find out why the Crows' attacks have grown so frequent, and where the Crows are coming from.

## Background

Until a year ago, the road between Millbrook and Harrowgate had the usual trouble: a robbery now and then, a stolen horse. Then a warlord who calls himself **the Rook** took over Ashwatch, an old toll fort on an abandoned road deep in the Ashwood, and gathered the scattered bandits of the region into one gang, the Crows. Every gang that wouldn't join him was wiped out.

**Why the attacks have grown (the truth for the DM):** The Rook wants to be the only boss in the region: every gang, merchant, and town paying him and answering to him. He sees himself as a king. He calls himself the King of the Ashwood, sits on a throne in the fort's old toll hall, and calls the Crows his court. His gang has grown from a dozen to about forty, and he must feed and pay them all, so he needs more toll every month. Marsh Freight's refusal to pay is a challenge to his rule, which is why he ordered Tobin made an example of.

## 1. Arrival in Harrowgate

Harrowgate is a walled market town at the east end of the Old Trade Road. The caravan arrives at the west gate as the lamps are being lit. Marsh Freight's yard is just inside.

**Hester Marsh** comes out to meet the caravan, sees Petra on the lead wagon, and understands before anyone speaks.

> She stands very still. "Where is he?" Then, quieter: "Did he pay them?"

Hester listens to the whole story without interrupting. Then she pays the party what Tobin promised: 40 gp each, or 50 gp if they negotiated a better fee.

### Hester's reaction

What Hester does next depends on what the party did with Tobin's body in [Act 1](../../01-the-caravan/adventures/01-guarding-the-caravan.md#travel-scene-what-now-day-2-after-the-ford).

| The party... | Hester's reaction | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| **Carried his body to Harrowgate** with care | She goes to the wagon, lifts the cloth from his face, and stays with him a long moment. When she turns back, her eyes are wet but steady. "You brought him home. I won't forget that." She invites the party to the burial at dawn the day after tomorrow, and from now on she trusts them completely. | **25 gp each**, and **150 XP each** (or Heroic Inspiration for each character, if you use milestone levelling) |
| **Buried him by the road** | She asks exactly where, and nods. "Then he's resting on the road he loved. I'll bring him home myself when it's safe." She is grateful, if quieter. | **10 gp each** |
| **Left his body** | She says nothing for a long moment. "I see." She pays the agreed fee, exactly, and turns away. Next morning she is cool and businesslike with the party. She still needs them, but they will have to earn her trust. | **None** |

**Other things the party can give Hester:**

- **Tobin's last words** ("Tell Hester... I wouldn't pay. Tell her it was Sable..."). Her grief turns to resolve.
- **The wooden horse** for her daughter, Lissy. She has to step away for a moment. If the party left Tobin's body, but brings her the horse, she softens a little: "So you didn't forget him entirely."
- **Lost wagons.** If wagons were burned, Hester notes it but doesn't hold it against the party. "Salt can be bought. Tobin can't."

Hester tells the party she has rooms paid for them at the Iron Harrow, and asks them to meet her there in the morning. "There's someone you should talk to. Get some sleep."

## 2. The night at the Iron Harrow

The **Iron Harrow** is a solid stone inn on Harrowgate's market square, named for the old iron harrow blade bolted above its door. Hester has paid for the party's rooms, meals, and baths for as long as they work for her.

The common room is busy, and the talk is all about the Crows. Morwen Tull, the innkeeper, is happy to talk to people who have actually fought them. Each character who spends some time in the common room hears one rumor (roll a d6 or choose).

| d6 | Rumor | True? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Half the council wants to pay the Rook just to make it stop." | True |
| 2 | "There were barely a dozen Crows last spring. Now they say there's forty, maybe more." | True |
| 3 | "The Rook's a fallen knight. Or a dragon in disguise. Depends who you ask." | False |
| 4 | "Woodcutters say they see lanterns flashing in the treetops deep in the Ashwood at night." | True: the Crows' lookouts signal to the fort |
| 5 | "Old Fennick's boy went off to join the Crows. Says the pay's better than mucking stables." | True: the Rook is recruiting |
| 6 | "There's an old toll fort deep in the Ashwood, on the forest road they abandoned fifty years ago. Nobody goes there anymore." | True, but someone does now |

**The feather on the door.** In the morning, the party finds a crow-feather token nailed to one of their doors. Nobody saw who left it. The Rook knows who they are.

## 3. The morning meeting

Over breakfast in a private room at the Iron Harrow, Hester (dressed in mourning) introduces **Captain Roderick Vane** of the Harrowgate guard.

> The captain lowers himself into a chair with a grunt and sets a battered map case on the table. "Mistress Marsh tells me you've met the Crows. Three times in three days. That's more than my whole watch has managed in a year."

Captain Vane is blunt about the problem:

- A year ago, the road saw an attack every month or two. In the last three months, there have been **fourteen attacks**, and they are getting bolder.
- He has twenty guards to protect the town walls, the market, and the jail. He can't patrol three days of road, let alone the Ashwood.
- The town council is split. Some councillors want to pay the Rook's toll just to keep trade moving. Vane thinks that would only be the start: "You pay a man like that once, and he owns you."
- He doesn't know where the Crows are based, how many there are, or why they have suddenly grown so bold.

**The request.** Hester and the captain ask the party to **find out why the bandits are attacking so much, and where they are coming from**. Once the party knows that, the town can decide what to do, and if the party can end the threat themselves, so much the better.

**The offer:**

- **The council's bounty:** 200 gp for proof that the Crows are broken (the Rook captured or dead, or the gang scattered).
- **Marsh Freight** adds 100 gp, and meals and rooms at the Iron Harrow for as long as the job takes.
- **Captain Vane** gives the party a writ naming them as deputies of the Harrowgate guard. It lets them question prisoners in the town jail and requisition mounts and supplies from the guard stables.

**What they can give the party to start:** Vane's map case holds a map of the region marked with every attack in the last year. The marks crowd closer to Harrowgate month by month, and none are north of the road. If the party has a captured Crow, Vane has them in the jail. If the party has no leads at all, he has a Crow his guards caught last week.

## 4. The prisoner

Captain Vane takes the party to the town jail beneath the guardhouse. If the party brought a captured Crow from Act 1, that's who they question; use that Crow's own personality with the rules below. Otherwise, the prisoner is **Jory Fennick**, a Crow the guard caught last week.

**Jory Fennick** is seventeen, skinny, and trying hard to look tough. He's the son of old Fennick the stablemaster (the rumor from the inn). He joined the Crows three months ago for the pay, and he regrets it. He is more afraid of the Rook than of the hangman: "You don't know what he does to talkers."

### The interrogation (skill challenge)

The party must convince Jory to talk, using **Charisma (Persuasion)** or **Charisma (Intimidation)** checks.

- **Goal:** 3 successes before 2 failures.
- **DC:** 13.
- **Each check** follows something a character says or does: an offer, a threat, an appeal. Let the players roleplay, then call for the check that fits. A character can't make two checks in a row.
- **Switching approach:** the first check after the party switches between Persuasion and Intimidation has Disadvantage, because Jory doesn't know whom to believe. A deliberate "good cop, bad cop" routine that the players set up in advance avoids this.

**Ways to gain Advantage on a check:**

- **His father.** If a character mentions old Fennick (from the inn rumor), or learns about him with a DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check, Persuasion checks have Advantage: "Your father's still waiting for you to come home."
- **Clemency.** Vane's writ lets the party promise Jory a lighter sentence. The first Persuasion check that offers it has Advantage.
- **Proof of strength.** Showing Jory crow-feather tokens taken from dead Crows, or Sable's ledger, gives Advantage on the first Intimidation check: the party is more dangerous than he thought.

**Success (3 successes).** Jory breaks down and tells them everything he knows:

- A Crow lieutenant called **Brannoc**, "the Magpie", runs a camp in the old charcoal burners' clearing at the eastern edge of the Ashwood, half a day from Harrowgate along the woodcutters' track. His crew raids the Harrowgate end of the road.
- Brannoc has four bandits and a scout in a tree stand. The watch changes at midnight.
- The password: the challenge is "Black wings at dusk", and the reply is "Coin for the Rook".
- "Brannoc knows the way to the Rook's fort. He goes there to report. He knows everything, and he keeps the Rook's letters hidden somewhere in camp. Not in his tent; he's not stupid."

**Failure (2 failures).** Jory clams up. "I'm not saying anything. Brannoc would gut me." Captain Vane recognizes the name: woodcutters have reported smoke near the old charcoal clearing at the edge of the Ashwood. The party learns where the camp is, but not the password or the watch change, and finding the clearing takes a DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) check (on a failure, they arrive after dark, tired, and the scout has Advantage on checks to notice them).

**Afterward.** If the party promised Jory clemency, Vane keeps the promise. If the party used threats or violence beyond what Vane allows (your call), Vane has a quiet word with them about how the Harrowgate guard does things.

## 5. Into the Ashwood

The woodcutters' track leaves the Old Trade Road a mile west of Harrowgate and winds into the eastern Ashwood. It is half a day's walk to the charcoal burners' clearing, or a couple of hours on the guard's horses.

**On the way:** [The Bear](../encounters/01-the-bear.md). *Low (200 XP).* At a stream crossing, a brown bear sow is fishing with her two cubs. The party can go around her, calm her (DC 14 Wisdom (Animal Handling)), scare her off, or fight. She flees when she drops to 11 HP. Harming her cubs makes the woods go quiet, and gives the camp's scout Advantage to notice the party.

**The camp:** [The Crow Camp](../encounters/02-the-crow-camp.md). *Moderate (900 XP).* Brannoc (a warrior veteran), four bandits, and a scout in a tree stand. The party can:

- **sneak in** (Stealth against the scout's passive Perception of 15, with Advantage at the midnight watch change);
- **walk in with the password**, claiming to bring a message from Sable (DC 12 Charisma (Deception) to keep up the story);
- **assault the camp**; or
- **cause a distraction** by setting fire to the tents or scattering the horses.

Brannoc fears the Rook more than the party, so he fights hard. To question him, the party must **keep him alive**: knock him out instead of killing him, or make him surrender once his crew is down. If he dies, the party must find his **hidden stash** of documents instead (DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation), 10 minutes per attempt; Crows returning from a raid arrive after the third failed attempt).

**What the party learns** (from Brannoc or the stash):

- **The Rook wants to be the only boss in the region, and he sees himself as a king.** He calls himself the King of the Ashwood, sits on a throne in his fort's hall, and calls the Crows his court.
- **His base is Ashwatch**, an old toll fort on the abandoned forest road deep in the Ashwood, a day's march north of the camp. A lookout in a ruined watchtower on the old road signals the fort with lanterns and a horn.

From Brannoc himself, the party can also learn that about twenty Crows are at the fort at any time, that an old culvert runs under the east wall, and that Sable is the Rook's right hand.

## 6. After the camp

The camp is only half a day from Harrowgate, so the party will probably go back before heading deeper into the Ashwood.

- **Captain Vane** takes Brannoc into custody (if he's alive) and hears what the party learned. If they found the stash, he reads the Rook's letters, signed "King of the Ashwood", grimly. "A king. He thinks he's a king." He offers the party **four guards** for the attack on the fort (use the Guard stat block from SRD 5.2), or supplies and horses if they prefer to go alone.
- **Hester Marsh** is told about the stolen goods. She sends wagons to bring them back, and the merchants whose goods are returned add **50 gp** to the party's bounty.
- **The town council**, shown the Rook's letters, stops arguing about paying the toll. Nobody wants to be a self-made king's subject.
