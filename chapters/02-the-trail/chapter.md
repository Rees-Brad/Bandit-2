# Act 2: The Trail

**Session 2. Level 3.**

In Harrowgate, the town offers a bounty to whoever stops the raids. The party follows the leads from Act 1 into the hills, finds the Crows' lookout post, and learns where the Rook is hiding and how his fort is guarded.

## Place in the story

- **Comes from:** [Act 1: The Caravan](../01-the-caravan/chapter.md).
- **Leads to:** [Act 3: The Bandit Lord](../03-the-bandit-lord/chapter.md). The party knows where the fort is and how it is guarded, and may have prisoners, a disguise, or an inside contact.
