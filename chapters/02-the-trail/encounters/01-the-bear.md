# The Bear

**Act 2, on the way to the Crow camp. Low difficulty (200 XP for four level 3 characters).**

On the woodcutters' track to the Crow camp, the party meets a brown bear protecting her cubs. She is dangerous, but the party doesn't have to fight her.

## Setup

The track crosses a shallow stream in a gully thick with brambles. A brown bear sow is fishing in the stream, with two cubs splashing in the shallows behind her. The wind is blowing toward the party, so she hasn't smelled them yet.

- A character who succeeds on a DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check spots the bear before the party walks into the open. Otherwise, the party is 40 feet from her when she rears up and roars.
- The gully sides are steep and thick with brambles. Climbing out is Difficult Terrain.
- The cubs are Tiny and harmless. They run behind their mother.

## Avoiding the fight

If the party spots her first or backs off when she roars, the fight can be avoided:

- **Going around:** a detour upstream costs an hour. A DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) check finds the way without losing the track.
- **Calming her:** a DC 14 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check, made by a character who backs away slowly. On a failure, she charges.
- **Food:** throwing her rations or fresh meat gives Advantage on the Animal Handling check.
- **Scaring her off:** fire, a loud noise, or magic, plus a DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation) check. On a success, she herds her cubs away.

## Tactics

- The bear attacks anyone who comes within 20 feet of her cubs, or anyone who harms them.
- She doesn't pursue anyone who retreats out of the gully.
- **Morale.** When she drops to 11 HP or fewer, she grabs a cub and lumbers off into the trees.
- **Harming the cubs** turns the party's Crow-camp approach sour: the woods go quiet, and the camp's scout is more alert (Advantage on Perception checks to notice the party).

## Scaling

- **Weaker party (level 1–2):** She flees at 15 HP.
- **Stronger party (level 4–5):** Her mate is nearby and joins on round 2 (two brown bears, 400 XP).
