# The Crow Camp

**Act 2. Moderate difficulty (900 XP for four level 3 characters).**

At the eastern edge of the Ashwood, half a day from Harrowgate, the Crow lieutenant **Brannoc**, called "the Magpie", runs a forward camp. His crew raids the Harrowgate end of the road. Brannoc knows who the Rook really is and where his fort lies. The party can get that from him if they **keep him alive and question him**, or from the **hidden stash** of documents he keeps in the camp.

## Setup

The camp is in an old charcoal burners' clearing, about 80 feet across, ringed by tall trees.

- **Three tents.** Brannoc's large tent is on the north side, with two small tents for his crew.
- **A cookfire** in the middle, where most of the crew spend the evening.
- **Stolen goods** under oiled tarps on the east side: crates and bales, some stamped with Marsh Freight's mark.
- **Four horses** on a picket line on the west side.
- **A tree stand** 20 feet up an oak on the south side, where a **scout** keeps watch (passive Perception 15). Climbing to it takes a DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check.

**Who is here:** Brannoc, four bandits, and the scout. In the evening, the bandits sit around the fire. After midnight, two of them sleep.

## Approaches

How the party approaches depends on what they learned from the prisoner in [Following the Trail](../adventures/01-following-the-trail.md#4-the-prisoner):

- **Sneaking in:** each character makes a Dexterity (Stealth) check against the scout's passive Perception of 15. If the party knows the watch changes at midnight, they can arrive then and have Advantage on the checks. If everyone succeeds, the party has Surprise.
- **Walking in with the password:** the challenge is "Black wings at dusk", and the reply is "Coin for the Rook". A character who gives the reply and claims to bring a message from Sable gets the party into the camp's clearing. Keeping up the story takes a DC 12 Charisma (Deception) check. If it fails, Brannoc gets suspicious and the fight starts with the party in the middle of the camp.
- **A straight assault:** the scout raises the alarm, and the Crows grab their weapons. Roll initiative normally.
- **Fire or the horses:** setting fire to the tents or scattering the horses (a DC 12 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check or simply cutting the picket line) pulls half the bandits away from the fight for 2 rounds.

If the party harmed the bear's cubs on the way, the scout has Advantage on Perception checks to notice them.

## Brannoc, "the Magpie"

Brannoc is the Rook's lieutenant for the Harrowgate end of the road. He's in his forties, lean, with a neat black beard and fingers full of stolen rings. Vain, clever, and greedy, he got his nickname because he can't resist anything shiny. He's loyal to the Rook only as long as it pays. He doesn't want to die for him.

## Tactics

- Brannoc fights from in front of his tent with his greatsword. He uses Parry, and calls his crew to surround the party's weakest-looking fighter.
- The bandits fire crossbows from behind the cookfire's woodpile (Half Cover), then close in.
- The scout shoots from the tree stand (Three-Quarters Cover).
- **Morale.** The bandits run when half of them are down. Brannoc fears the Rook more than he fears the party, and he fights hard.

## Keeping Brannoc alive

The party has to decide to take Brannoc alive. Remind the players that he's the one who knows the way to the Rook, if they seem to have forgotten.

- **Knocking him out.** A character who reduces Brannoc to 0 Hit Points with a melee attack can knock him out instead of killing him.
- **Making him surrender.** Once all his crew are down or fled and he is Bloodied (32 HP or fewer), a character can use an action to demand his surrender with a DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation) check, or a DC 13 check if they promise him his life. On a success, he drops his sword: "Fine! Fine. You want the Rook? I'll give you the Rook."
- **If Brannoc dies**, the party must find his [hidden stash](#the-hidden-stash) to learn what he knew.

### Questioning Brannoc

A captured Brannoc talks to save his own neck. He tells the truth about the things below, but lies about anything that makes him look bad (a DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check notices when he's lying).

### What Brannoc knows

- **What the Rook wants.** "He doesn't want to be a bandit. He wants to be the only boss there is. Every gang in the Ashwood either joined him or ended up in a ditch. Every merchant pays him, and the towns will be next." The Rook sees himself as a king. He calls himself the **King of the Ashwood**, sits on a throne built from the old toll gate in the fort's hall, and makes the Crows kneel and call him "Majesty". "He means it. That's what scares me."
- **Where his base is.** The Rook holds **Ashwatch**, an old toll fort on the forest road that was abandoned fifty years ago, deep in the Ashwood. It's a day's march north of the camp along the overgrown road.
- **The lookout.** An hour short of the fort, a lookout in a ruined watchtower on the old road signals the fort with lanterns by night and a horn by day. If the party doesn't silence it, the fort will be warned.
- **Numbers.** There are about forty Crows. At any time, half of them are out raiding, so twenty or so are in the fort.
- **The way in.** An old drainage culvert runs under the fort's east wall, barred with a rusted iron grate. The password at the gate is the same as at the camp.
- **Sable.** She's the Rook's right hand, and Brannoc hates her. If she's still alive, she's at the fort.

**What Brannoc lies about:** he says he never killed anyone (he has), and that the culvert grate is easy to open (it takes a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check, or a DC 13 Dexterity check with thieves' tools to pick the lock that holds it).

## The hidden stash

Brannoc keeps the Rook's letters where his own crew can't read them: in a hollow under the roots of the oak that holds the scout's tree stand, covered with a flat stone and leaf litter.

**Finding it.** Each search of the camp takes 10 minutes and needs a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check. These things help:

- **Brannoc's eyes.** If Brannoc is captured, a character who succeeds on a DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check while he's being questioned notices him glancing at the oak. The next search has Advantage.
- **A captured bandit** knows Brannoc "goes off by the big oak at night", and says so with a DC 10 Charisma (Intimidation) or Charisma (Persuasion) check.
- **Jory's tip.** If the party won the interrogation, they know the papers aren't in the tent.

**Taking too long.** After the **third failed search**, three Crows returning from a raid (two bandits and a tough) walk into the camp. Once they are dealt with, the party finds the stash without a further check.

**What's in the stash** (a waxed leather pouch):

- **Letters from the Rook**, signed "**Rook, King of the Ashwood**" with the squat tower seal. They order more raids "until every merchant in my kingdom pays his king", and promise Brannoc a lordship "when I am crowned".
- **A tally of tribute** paid into "the King's treasury" by other gangs, merchants, and farms. The list is long.
- **A map** of the abandoned forest road north to **Ashwatch**, with the lookout's ruined watchtower marked.
- **A slip of paper** with this month's password.

Together, these tell the party what Brannoc would: the Rook wants to be the only boss in the region and sees himself as a king, and his base is Ashwatch, the old fort in the woods.

## Brannoc's tent

Brannoc's tent holds his bedroll, fine clothes, and a strongbox (locked; DC 13 Dexterity check with thieves' tools, or break it open with a DC 15 Strength check) with 85 gp, 140 sp, and three gold rings worth 15 gp each. There are no papers.

## The stolen goods

The crates and bales under the tarps are worth about 300 gp. Some are Marsh Freight's, taken in earlier raids. Returning them to Harrowgate takes the horses and a wagon from the town. Hester and the other merchants are grateful (see the [adventure](../adventures/01-following-the-trail.md#6-after-the-camp)).

## Scaling

- **Weaker party (level 1–2):** Use the Bandit Captain stat block from SRD 5.2 for Brannoc instead (as Sable's, in [Act 1](../../01-the-caravan/encounters/02-the-midway-ambush.md)), and remove two bandits (600 XP).
- **Stronger party (level 4–5):** Add two toughs and a second scout (1,200 XP).
