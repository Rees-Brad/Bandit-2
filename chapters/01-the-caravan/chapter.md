# Act 1: The Caravan

**Session 1. Level 3.**

In a Millbrook tavern, coach driver Tobin Marsh hires the party to guard his three-wagon caravan on its three-day journey along the Old Trade Road to Harrowgate. His cousin Hester runs Marsh Freight there. Marsh Freight refused to pay the Rook's toll, so the Crows attack three times: just outside Millbrook, at Crow's Ford halfway along the road, and on the last hill before Harrowgate. Tobin is killed in the second attack, and the party must bring the caravan and the news to Hester.

## Place in the story

- **Comes from:** The adventure hook (see [campaign.md](../../campaign.md#using-this-adventure)).
- **Leads to:** [Act 2: The Trail](../02-the-trail/chapter.md), which begins as the caravan reaches Harrowgate. Note what the party did with Tobin's body; it decides Hester's reaction. The party brings leads from the attacks: crow-feather tokens, Sable's toll ledger, the raiders' tracks, or a captured Crow.
