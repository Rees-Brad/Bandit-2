# Attack 3: Before Harrowgate

**Act 1, Day 3. Low to Moderate difficulty (500 XP, or 950 XP if Sable leads).**

On the last hill before Harrowgate, with the town walls in sight, the Crows try once more. This time they aren't trying to rob the caravan. They want to **burn it**, so everyone in Harrowgate sees what happens to anyone who defies the Rook.

## Setup

The road drops through a sunken lane, a cutting 10 feet deep and 15 feet wide with steep earth banks. At the bottom, a hay cart has been dragged across the road and set alight. Crows wait on top of both banks with torches and crossbows.

- **Climbing a bank** takes a DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check or 10 feet of extra movement for each 5 feet climbed.
- Characters in the lane are 10 feet below the Crows, who have Half Cover along the bank tops.
- **The burning cart** blocks the road. Moving it takes a DC 14 Strength (Athletics) check (a character who enters its space or starts their turn there takes 3 (1d6) Fire damage). Two characters working together have Advantage.

**Who leads:** If Sable escaped [Attack 2](02-the-midway-ambush.md), she leads this attack and wants revenge. If not, the toughs are leaderless and quicker to break.

## Burning the wagons

As an action, a bandit or tough within 30 feet of a wagon can throw a torch at it (*Ranged Attack Roll:* +3 against AC 10). On a hit, the wagon catches fire.

- A burning wagon is destroyed, with its cargo, at the end of its third round on fire.
- A character can use an action to put out a burning wagon: smother it with a tarp, beat out the flames, or throw water on it. That ends the fire.
- The teamsters will cut the horses free, but they won't fight.

How much cargo survives decides how Hester Marsh greets the party in Harrowgate (see the [adventure](../adventures/01-guarding-the-caravan.md#arrival-in-harrowgate)).

## Help from Harrowgate

The teamster Petra carries a signal horn. If someone blows it (an action), the Harrowgate gate watch sees the smoke, hears the horn, and rides out. Six guards arrive at the start of the fifth round, and any Crows still standing flee.

## Tactics

- In round 1, the bandits throw torches at the wagons while the toughs and the scout shoot at the party.
- The Crows on top of the banks stay there. They only come down to finish off a downed character.
- Sable, if she is here, targets whoever stopped her at the ford.
- **Morale.** The Crows flee when half of them are down or when the guards arrive. If Sable leads, she fights until she is Bloodied (26 HP or fewer), then flees.

## What the party can learn

- **Tracks.** The Crows came over the hills, not along the road. A DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) check shows a well-used trail heading northeast into the hills, the trail the party can follow in Act 2.
- **A captured Crow** is frightened and will trade what they know for their life: the Crows have a lookout post on the hill trail, two days out, and the Rook's fort lies beyond it.

## Scaling

- **Weaker party (level 1–2):** Remove two toughs. If Sable leads, use her weaker version from Attack 2 (30 HP).
- **Stronger party (level 4–5):** Add two toughs and a scout. If Sable doesn't lead, a tough boss leads instead.

Sable is built from the SRD 5.2 Bandit Captain. **Change:** her Pistol is replaced with a Hand Crossbow.
