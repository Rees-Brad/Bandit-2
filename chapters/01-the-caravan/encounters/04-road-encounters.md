# Road Encounters

**Act 1, optional. Random encounters on the Old Trade Road.**

The road between Millbrook and Harrowgate runs through farmland, the Ashwood, and open hills. Use this table to add danger between the three Crow attacks.

## When to roll

Roll a d6 once each day and once each night of the journey. On a 1, roll on the table below, or pick an entry that suits the moment.

The party already faces three scripted attacks, so **use no more than one random encounter a day** for a party of level 3. Keep the fights short. Their job is to wear down resources and break up the travel, not to replace the Crows.

## 1. Wolf pack

Four hungry wolves circle the camp after dark, looking for a horse to take. They go for the picketed horses first and fight anyone who gets between them and the herd. When two wolves are down, the rest flee.

- **Weaker party:** 2 wolves. **Stronger party:** 6 wolves, or add a dire wolf.

## 2. Dire wolf

A dire wolf and two wolves stalk the caravan, staying at the edge of the light. A DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check spots their eyes before they rush in. The dire wolf knocks a target Prone, and the wolves pile on with Pack Tactics. The pack flees if the dire wolf dies.

- **Weaker party:** the dire wolf alone. **Stronger party:** 2 dire wolves and 2 wolves.

## 3. Wild boars

A sounder of boars bursts out of the undergrowth across the road, and the wagon horses panic. The driver of each wagon makes a DC 12 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check (a character can take the reins and make it instead); on a failure, the wagon lurches off the road and is stuck until someone spends 10 minutes freeing it. The boars charge anyone nearby, then scatter when two are down.

- **Weaker party:** 2 boars. **Stronger party:** 6 boars.

## 4. Brown bear

A brown bear is feeding on a dead deer in the middle of the road. It won't give up the carcass. The party can go around it through the trees (an hour's delay to clear a path for the wagons), drive it off (a DC 15 Wisdom (Animal Handling) or Charisma (Intimidation) check, or offering it food), or fight it. Once it drops to half its HP (11), it lumbers away.

- **Weaker party:** the bear flees at 15 HP. **Stronger party:** 2 brown bears (a mother and a grown cub).

## 5. Giant spiders

Thick webs stretch between the trees across the road, with old wrapped bundles hanging from them. Two giant spiders wait in the branches above (spotting them takes a Wisdom (Perception) check against DC 17, or a passive Perception of 17 or higher). Clearing the web for the wagons takes 10 minutes or some fire. The spiders attack whoever touches the web first. One bundle holds a dead traveler wearing a crow-feather token: a Crow who wandered off from the gang.

- **Weaker party:** 1 giant spider. **Stronger party:** 3 giant spiders.

## 6. Goblin raiders

A goblin war band has come down from the hills, trying to get its share of the road before the Crows take all of it. Four goblin warriors shoot from the rocks, using Nimble Escape to hide between shots, while the goblin boss waits for an opening to grab a crate. The goblins hate the Crows. A captured goblin will happily say that "the tower men" drove the goblins out of the deep Ashwood, and point north into the trees.

- **Weaker party:** 3 goblin warriors and no boss. **Stronger party:** 6 goblin warriors and the boss.

## 7. Ravens on the dead

A wrecked wagon lies beside the road with dead travelers, a family that wouldn't pay the toll. Flocks of ravens are feeding on them and rise screaming at anyone who comes near. The wreck carries a painted warning: **"PAY THE ROOK."** The party can bury the dead. A DC 10 Wisdom (Insight) check makes it clear the teamsters are badly shaken; their morale is low for the rest of the trip.

- **Weaker party:** 2 swarms. **Stronger party:** 5 swarms.

## 8. The robbed peddler

A tinker named Oda walks the road toward Millbrook, leading a donkey with empty saddlebags. The Crows took everything she had. She tells the party that the Crows "have eyes all along the road", that a lieutenant called Sable runs the ambushes, and that the Rook "thinks he's a king, and the rest of us are his peasants". If the party gives her food or coin, she adds that she has seen lanterns flashing in the treetops deep in the Ashwood at night, like signals.
