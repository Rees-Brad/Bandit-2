# Attack 2: The Midway Ambush

**Act 1, Day 2. Moderate difficulty (950 XP for four level 3 characters).**

In the middle of the Ashwood, the road crosses a shallow river at Crow's Ford. The Rook's lieutenant, **Sable**, has set a real ambush here to make an example of Marsh Freight. **Tobin Marsh is killed in the first volley.**

## Setup

The ford is 40 feet wide and knee-deep, with a gravel bed. Water and mud count as Difficult Terrain, and the wagons can only move at a crawl. On the far bank, a freshly felled tree lies across the road. Thick woods come down to the water on both sides.

- **Far bank:** Sable, two toughs, and two bandits are hidden behind the fallen tree (Half Cover).
- **Near bank:** Two bandits wait in the trees behind the caravan to cut off its retreat.
- **Up in the trees:** Two scouts, one on each bank, are 15 feet up (Three-Quarters Cover, +5 AC). Climbing to them is a DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check.

Spotting the ambush before it starts takes a Wisdom (Perception) check against DC 16. A success means the noticing character isn't Surprised. **It does not save Tobin**; see below.

## The driver's death

This death is scripted. Run it as narration, not as dice rolls.

> As the lead wagon rolls into the water, crossbows snap from the far bank. Every bolt flies at the driver's bench. Tobin jerks and slumps sideways. The reins slide from his hands into the river.

Tobin is dying. Any healing magic or a DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check keeps him conscious just long enough to speak once the fight is over. He does not survive. His last words:

> "Tell Hester... I wouldn't pay. Tell her it was Sable. The tower on the feathers. Don't let him win."

Make it clear the party couldn't have prevented this. On the Rook's orders, Sable aimed every crossbow at Tobin, to show what happens to anyone who refuses the toll.

## Tactics

- In the first round, the bandits and toughs fire at Tobin (see above). From then on they fire at the party.
- Sable stays behind the tree, shouts orders, and fires her hand crossbow. She only moves into melee to finish off a weakened character.
- The near-bank bandits attack the rear wagon's teamsters if nobody is guarding them. This pulls the party in two directions.
- **Morale.** When Sable is Bloodied (26 HP or fewer), she runs for a horse tethered 100 feet into the woods on the far bank, and the rest of the Crows scatter. If she gets away, she leads [Attack 3](03-before-harrowgate.md).

## Talking instead

Sable doesn't negotiate. She has her orders. A captured Crow can be questioned after the fight.

## What the party can learn

- **Sable's saddlebag** (if she is caught, or a DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) check follows her trail back to the horse before she reaches it) holds a **toll ledger**. It lists the caravans that paid and the ones that refused, and Marsh Freight is circled. The back page has a rough sketch of a trail leading north from the road into the Ashwood, marked with the same squat tower as the crow-feather tokens. This is the strongest lead for Act 2.
- **A captured Crow** knows Sable is the Rook's right hand and that she reports to "the old fort in the woods". They don't know the way there; scouts guide the new recruits.

## Scaling

- **Weaker party (level 1–2):** Remove the two scouts and both toughs. Give Sable 30 HP and have her flee when she drops to 15 HP (550 XP).
- **Stronger party (level 4–5):** Add two toughs and two more scouts (1,350 XP).

### Sable (Bandit Captain) (1)

Sable is built from the SRD 5.2 Bandit Captain. **Change:** her Pistol is replaced with a Hand Crossbow, so she fits settings without firearms. If your setting has firearms, give her back the Pistol (*Ranged Attack Roll:* +5, range 30/90 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) Piercing damage).

*Medium or Small Humanoid, Neutral*

- **AC** 15 · **Initiative** +3 (13)
- **HP** 52 (8d8 + 16)
- **Speed** 30 ft.

| Str | Dex | Con | Int | Wis | Cha |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) |

- **Saving Throws** Str +4, Dex +5, Con +2, Int +2, Wis +2, Cha +2
- **Skills** Athletics +4, Deception +4
- **Gear** Hand Crossbow, Scimitar, Studded Leather Armor
- **Senses** Passive Perception 10
- **Languages** Common, Thieves' Cant

**Actions**

- ***Multiattack.*** Sable makes two attacks, using Scimitar and Hand Crossbow in any combination.

- ***Hand Crossbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +5, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Piercing damage.

**Reactions**

- ***Parry.*** *Trigger:* Sable is hit by a melee attack roll while holding a weapon. *Response:* Sable adds 2 to her AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
