# Attack 1: Outside Millbrook

**Act 1, Day 1. Low difficulty (450 XP for four level 3 characters).**

Less than an hour out of Millbrook, the Crows block the road and demand the Rook's toll. This is a test: they want to see who is guarding the caravan and how well they fight.

## Setup

The road climbs out of the river valley between hedgerows and a stony hillside. Around a bend, a handcart lies on its side across the road. Six bandits stand behind it with crossbows. Their leader, a big, cheerful thug called **Gully**, steps forward with his hands open.

> "Morning! This is the Rook's road now, friends. Toll's one wagon in three, or fifty gold a wagon, your pick. Marsh Freight was told already. Last chance."

Two **scouts** lie hidden on the hillside 60 feet above the road (Stealth +6; spotting them takes a Wisdom (Perception) check against DC 16, or a passive Perception of 16 or higher). They open fire if a fight starts.

Tobin refuses to pay. He's been told about the toll before and won't give in. Unless the party comes up with another plan, the fight starts.

## Tactics

- The bandits shoot from behind the cart (Half Cover, +2 AC) and only close in to melee when a character reaches them.
- Gully closes in to melee and uses Pack Tactics.
- The scouts shoot at whoever looks most dangerous, then at the horses (AC 10, HP 15) to stop the wagons.
- **Morale.** When Gully goes down or half the Crows are out of the fight, the rest scatter into the hills. They are testing the party, not making a last stand.

## Talking instead

Paying the toll ends the encounter, but Tobin is furious, and the Crows will be back at the midpoint anyway (the Rook wants to make an example of Marsh Freight). A character who succeeds on a DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation) check, after showing some real force, makes Gully back down for today.

## What the party can learn

- **A captured bandit** knows that the Crows work for the Rook, a warlord who wants to control "every coin that moves between the two towns". They know Marsh Freight refused to pay and is to be made an example of. They also know a lieutenant called **Sable** is waiting farther down the road. They don't know where the Rook's fort is; only Sable's crew go there.
- **Every Crow** wears a black feather threaded through a copper ring stamped with a squat tower. This **crow-feather token** comes up again in Act 2.

## Scaling

- **Weaker party (level 1–2):** Remove the scouts and two bandits (200 XP).
- **Stronger party (level 4–5):** Add two toughs and two bandits, and give Gully the Tough Boss stat block (SRD 5.2) instead.
