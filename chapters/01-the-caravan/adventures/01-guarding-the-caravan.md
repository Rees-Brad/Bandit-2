# Guarding the Caravan

**Act 1. One session. Four or five characters of level 3.**

A coach driver hires the party in a Millbrook tavern to guard his caravan on the three-day journey to Harrowgate. The Crows, the gang of a warlord called the Rook, attack three times along the way. In the second attack, the driver is killed. The party must bring the caravan, and the news, to his cousin in Harrowgate.

## Background

The Rook is a greedy warlord who wants to control the region and every coin of trade that moves through it. From an old toll fort in the hills, his gang, the Crows, have taken over the Old Trade Road between Millbrook and Harrowgate. They make every traveler pay "the Rook's toll": one wagon in three, or fifty gold a wagon.

**Marsh Freight**, a small caravan company based in Harrowgate, refused to pay. The Rook has decided to make an example of them. Its owner, **Hester Marsh**, has sent her cousin **Tobin** to bring a caravan back from Millbrook. Tobin knows the Crows will come for him, so he is looking for guards.

## 1. The tavern in Millbrook

The party is at the **Plough and Lantern**, a busy tavern by Millbrook's east gate where teamsters and travelers drink before taking the road.

> A broad-shouldered man in a dusty driving coat pushes through the crowd to your table, hat in hand. "Tobin Marsh, Marsh Freight. You look like folk who can handle yourselves. I've got three wagons leaving for Harrowgate at first light, and I need guards. There's bandits on the road, and they've got a grudge against my company."

**What Tobin offers:** 10 gp each now and 40 gp each when the caravan reaches Harrowgate, paid by his cousin Hester, who runs the company. Meals on the road are included. A character who succeeds on a DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check raises the payment on arrival to 50 gp each. Tobin isn't a rich man, but he knows he needs help.

**What Tobin tells them:**

- The bandits call themselves the Crows. They work for someone called "the Rook" and demand a toll from anyone on the road.
- Marsh Freight refused to pay. "It's our road too. My grandfather drove it before any Rook was hatched."
- The wagons carry wool cloth, salt, and iron tools: honest goods Harrowgate needs.
- The journey takes three days: farmland, then the Ashwood, then the hills above Harrowgate.
- His cousin Hester runs the company. "She's the clever one. I'm just the one who drives."

**If the party turns him down,** Tobin leaves the caravan that morning anyway. A day later, the party hears the Crows burned his wagons on the road. If you need to bring them back in, Hester's letter arrives asking them to find her cousin's killers (start [Act 2](../../02-the-trail/chapter.md) directly).

## 2. The road

The journey takes three days. Use this timeline, adding [road encounters](../encounters/04-road-encounters.md) where you want them (no more than one a day for a level 3 party).

| When | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1, morning | Hills just outside Millbrook | [Attack 1: Outside Millbrook](../encounters/01-outside-millbrook.md). Gully demands the toll. |
| Day 1, afternoon and night | Farmland, roadside camp | Travel scene: Tobin's stories. Roll for a road encounter. |
| Day 2, midday | Crow's Ford in the Ashwood | [Attack 2: The Midway Ambush](../encounters/02-the-midway-ambush.md). **Tobin is killed.** |
| Day 2, afternoon and night | Ashwood | Travel scene: what now? Roll for a road encounter. |
| Day 3, late afternoon | The last hill before Harrowgate | [Attack 3: Before Harrowgate](../encounters/03-before-harrowgate.md). The Crows try to burn the caravan. |
| Day 3, evening | Harrowgate | Arrival. Hester Marsh. |

The party can take a Short Rest after each attack while the teamsters calm the horses and check the loads. They get a Long Rest each night unless a road encounter interrupts it.

### Travel scene: Tobin's stories (day 1)

Give the players a reason to like Tobin before he dies. Around the fire, he:

- tells how his grandfather drove this road with a single mule, and how Hester turned the family's one wagon into a company of twelve;
- asks each character where they're from and what they'll do with their pay;
- shows a small carved wooden horse he's bringing home for Hester's daughter;
- admits, quietly, that he's afraid, "but I'd be more afraid of a world where you pay a man like the Rook to use your own road."

### Travel scene: what now? (day 2, after the ford)

After Tobin's death, the teamsters are shaken.

- **Wil** wants to turn back to Millbrook. Petra wants to go on: "Tobin would've gone on." The party decides. Going back means a day and a half on the road the Crows know well, and so does going on.
- **Petra** takes Tobin's place at the reins. She asks the party to keep Tobin's body with the caravan so Hester can bury him.
- **The wooden horse** is in Tobin's coat pocket.
- A character who comforts the teamsters (a DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) check or a good bit of roleplay) steadies them. If nobody does, Wil slips away in the night and walks back to Millbrook, and the third wagon needs a new driver.

## 3. The three attacks

Each attack is written out in full in its encounter file, and the stat blocks are also in the [appendix](#appendix-stat-blocks) at the end of this file.

### Attack 1: Outside Millbrook

[Full encounter](../encounters/01-outside-millbrook.md). *Low (450 XP).*

Less than an hour out of town, an overturned handcart blocks the road. Gully (a tough) and six bandits demand the toll while two scouts hide on the hillside above (DC 16 to spot). Tobin refuses. The Crows fight from behind the cart and scatter when Gully drops or half of them are down. This is a test of the guards' strength. **Leads:** a captured bandit names the Rook and Sable; every Crow wears a crow-feather token stamped with a tower.

### Attack 2: The Midway Ambush

[Full encounter](../encounters/02-the-midway-ambush.md). *Moderate (950 XP).*

At Crow's Ford in the Ashwood, the wagons are stuck in knee-deep water when Sable (a bandit captain), two toughs, four bandits, and two scouts in the trees open fire. **Tobin is killed in the opening volley**; this is scripted, not rolled. If the party reaches him, he lives just long enough to say: *"Tell Hester... I wouldn't pay. Tell her it was Sable. The tower on the feathers. Don't let him win."* Sable flees on horseback when Bloodied. **Leads:** Sable's toll ledger, with a sketch of the hill trail on the back page.

### Attack 3: Before Harrowgate

[Full encounter](../encounters/03-before-harrowgate.md). *Low to Moderate (500 XP, or 950 XP with Sable).*

In a sunken lane on the last hill, with Harrowgate's walls in sight, the Crows block the road with a burning hay cart and throw torches at the wagons. A wagon on fire for three rounds is lost. Petra's signal horn brings the Harrowgate watch in round 5. If Sable escaped the ford, she leads this attack. **Leads:** the Crows' tracks lead northeast into the hills; a captured Crow knows about the lookout post.

## 4. Arrival in Harrowgate

Harrowgate is a walled market town at the end of the Old Trade Road. Marsh Freight's yard is just inside the west gate.

**Hester Marsh** comes out to meet the caravan, sees Petra on the lead wagon, and understands before anyone speaks.

> She stands very still. "Where is he?" Then, quieter: "Did he pay them?"

Hester is grief-stricken but practical. She pays what Tobin promised and asks to hear everything. How she reacts depends on the caravan:

- **All three wagons arrived:** She pays the full fee, and adds 10 gp each "because Tobin would have."
- **One or more wagons were lost:** She still pays the full fee. "You brought him home. That matters more than salt."
- **The party gives her the wooden horse** or delivers Tobin's last words: she breaks down for a moment, then her grief turns to resolve.

Hester doesn't want revenge. She wants the road back. She tells the party that the town council of Harrowgate is offering a bounty for whoever ends the Crows, and that Marsh Freight will add to it. "The Rook thinks he can own this road. Prove him wrong." This leads into [Act 2: The Trail](../../02-the-trail/chapter.md).

### The leads so far

Whatever happened, the party should leave Act 1 with at least one of these. If they have none, Hester has one: a Crow who was caught by the Harrowgate watch and is in the town jail.

| Lead | Where it comes from | Where it points |
|---|---|---|
| Crow-feather tokens stamped with a squat tower | Every Crow | The old toll fort in the hills |
| Sable's toll ledger, with a trail sketch | Sable's saddlebag (Attack 2) | The hill trail and the fort |
| Tracks heading northeast | Attack 3 site | The hill trail and the lookout post |
| A captured Crow | Any attack | The lookout post, two days into the hills |
| Oda the peddler's story | Road encounter 8 | Mirror signals from the hills to the northeast |

## Rewards

- **Pay:** 10 gp each up front and 40 gp (or 50 gp) each on arrival, plus Hester's bonus.
- **Loot:** Each Crow carries 1d6 sp and a crow-feather token. Gully carries 12 gp. Sable carries 25 gp and the toll ledger.
- **Experience:** If you use milestones, the party should be close to level 4 by the end of Act 2. For XP, the three attacks are worth 1,900–2,350 XP in total for the whole party.
