## Millbrook
- **Role:** Starting town. Tobin hires the party at the Plough and Lantern tavern.
- **Description:** A river-valley town at the west end of the Old Trade Road. Placeholder name; rename to fit your world.
- **Appears in:** [Guarding the Caravan](chapters/01-the-caravan/adventures/01-guarding-the-caravan.md#1-the-tavern-in-millbrook)

## Harrowgate
- **Role:** Destination of the caravan and home of Marsh Freight and Captain Vane's guard. Where Act 2 begins.
- **Description:** A walled market town at the east end of the Old Trade Road. Placeholder name.
- **Appears in:** [Following the Trail](chapters/02-the-trail/adventures/01-following-the-trail.md#1-arrival-in-harrowgate), [Attack 3: Before Harrowgate](chapters/01-the-caravan/encounters/03-before-harrowgate.md)

## The Old Trade Road
- **Role:** The road the Rook wants to control. Three days' travel through farmland, the Ashwood, and hills.
- **Description:** The only good wagon road between Millbrook and Harrowgate.
- **Appears in:** [Guarding the Caravan](chapters/01-the-caravan/adventures/01-guarding-the-caravan.md#2-the-road), [Road Encounters](chapters/01-the-caravan/encounters/04-road-encounters.md)

## Crow's Ford
- **Role:** Where Sable's ambush kills Tobin Marsh.
- **Description:** A knee-deep, 40-foot-wide river crossing in the middle of the Ashwood.
- **Appears in:** [Attack 2: The Midway Ambush](chapters/01-the-caravan/encounters/02-the-midway-ambush.md)

## The Iron Harrow
- **Role:** The inn on Harrowgate's market square where the party stays, paid for by Hester. Scene of the morning meeting.
- **Description:** A solid stone inn with an old iron harrow blade bolted above its door. Kept by Morwen Tull.
- **Appears in:** [Following the Trail](chapters/02-the-trail/adventures/01-following-the-trail.md#2-the-night-at-the-iron-harrow)

## The Crow camp
- **Role:** Brannoc's forward camp, from which the Crows raid the Harrowgate end of the road.
- **Description:** An old charcoal burners' clearing at the eastern edge of the Ashwood, half a day from Harrowgate along the woodcutters' track.
- **Appears in:** [The Crow Camp](chapters/02-the-trail/encounters/02-the-crow-camp.md)

## Ashwatch
- **Role:** The Rook's base. An old toll fort on an abandoned forest road deep in the Ashwood, a day's march north of the Crow camp. A lookout in a ruined watchtower on the old road guards the approach.
- **Description:** Abandoned fifty years ago when the road was moved. The Rook holds court on a throne in its old toll hall. Its mark, a squat tower, is stamped on every crow-feather token.
- **Appears in:** [The Crow Camp](chapters/02-the-trail/encounters/02-the-crow-camp.md#what-brannoc-knows), [Following the Trail](chapters/02-the-trail/adventures/01-following-the-trail.md#7-the-road-to-ashwatch), [The Bandit Lord's Lair](chapters/03-the-bandit-lord/adventures/01-the-bandit-lords-lair.md)
